Help & Support

Need help with your order or one of our products? Please see some of our frequently asked questions below.

How do I place an order?

Select a product from our Shop page and add it to your shopping cart. Check your order details, enter your shipping information and select a payment option to complete your order.

How much is shipping?

Standard shipping is available across New Zealand. Orders over $600 qualify for free shipping. Orders of $600 or less have a standard shipping charge.

Can I change my order?

If you need to change an order, please contact our customer support team as soon as possible. We will try to help before the order is shipped.

How can I contact you?

You can contact our support team by email or phone. Our team can help with product questions, orders and delivery information.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit and debit card payments. PayPal is also available as a payment option during checkout.

Can I return a product?

If you are not satisfied with your purchase, please contact us with your order details. Our support team will provide information about the return process.
