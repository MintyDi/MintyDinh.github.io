About Aotearoa Adventure Gear

Adventure Starts Here

Aotearoa Adventure Gear is a New Zealand outdoor equipment store providing quality products for people who enjoy exploring the outdoors.

Our store offers a range of equipment including hiking bags, camping tents, outdoor jackets and hiking accessories.

We aim to make outdoor shopping simple and convenient by providing useful products at competitive prices.

Our Story

Aotearoa Adventure Gear was created for outdoor enthusiasts who want reliable equipment for weekend trips, hiking adventures and camping holidays.

Our products are selected with the New Zealand outdoor environment in mind. Whether you are planning a short walk or a longer camping trip, we aim to provide practical equipment for your next adventure.
